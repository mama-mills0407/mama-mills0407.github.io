About Me

My Bio

Hi, I’m the mom behind Mom Life Picks. I started this site to share the products, routines, and little time-savers that have genuinely made family life easier for me.

Why I Started This Blog

Like a lot of moms, I found myself overwhelmed by endless product lists, must-have claims, and advice that didn’t always match real life. I wanted a place to keep things simple: practical recommendations, honest thoughts, and products that are actually worth it.

What You’ll Find Here

On Mom Life Picks, I focus on helpful finds for babies, moms, and busy homes. If something saves time, reduces stress, or makes everyday life a little easier, it’s probably the kind of thing I’ll share here.

A Little More About Me

I’m passionate about creating a home that feels calm, functional, and full of the little things that matter most. When I’m not writing or testing products, I’m usually doing the usual mom juggling act and trying to keep life organized one day at a time.
