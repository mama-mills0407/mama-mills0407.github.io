Pro Tips from a Diaper Veteran

  • Size matters: Don't try to stretch Size 1 forever. When leaks start happening, it's time to size up. Your mattress will thank you.
  • Watch for redness: If your baby develops a rash, try a different brand. Every baby's skin is different—it's not personal; it's just biology.
  • Bulk up: Buy diapers during sales and stock up. Your future self will be so grateful when you're not making an emergency 2 AM run.
  • Night mode activated: Regular diapers during the day, overnight diapers before bed. This simple swap changed my life (and my sheets).
