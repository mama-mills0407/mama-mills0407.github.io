10 Newborn Essentials for an Amazon Baby List

Creating a baby registry can feel like you're preparing for a moon landing. DO YOU REALLY NEED 47 TINY OUTFITS? (Spoiler: You do not.) Here are the essentials that will actually make your life easier, not just cluttered.

1. A Good Bassinet (Your New Favorite Piece of Furniture)

Halo BassinestSwivel Sleeper: Why do I love it? I don't want an item that will only be useful for a couple months. This item will make itself useful all the way into toddlerhood! The elevated bassinet bed is easily removed for once baby gets older. The bouncer can convert into a toddler chair. This is a great option for apartments or other small spaces, in place of a crib.

2. Blackout Curtains (Because Sleep Is Sacred)

NICETOWN Blackout Curtains: When your baby finally falls asleep, the last thing you need is the sunrise ruining everything. This serves as an essential for daytime naps for south facing windows that get bright and let in heat during nap time. These curtains turn your nursery into a cave. A cozy, baby-friendly cave.

3. A White Noise Machine (Shush, Baby, Shush)

Hatch 2: Sounds like you're living inside a fan? Your baby will sleep like they're in the womb again. There are a lot of different machines. This is my favorite due to its custom-ability of sounds and volume. Love that I can turn on and off from my phone. And it comes with a portable version that's long lasting. The minute I turn this baby on while out and about baby is soothed.

4. Swaddles (The Baby Burrito Starter Kit)

Soft Adjustable Sleep Sack: Personally we preferred Jersey knit swaddle blankets, right up until be had an escape artist. Then came the baby straight jacket.

5. A Diaper Pail (Odor? We Don't Know Her)

Diaper Genie Elite: When your husband says "it doesn't smell that bad," he's lying. This pail is a game-changer. Nobody wants a stinky nursery. Seal those stinky diapers away like they contain nuclear waste.

6. A Good Changing Pad (Protect Your Furniture)

Graco Contoured Changing Pad:We spent a little extra on this one. Being we had a premie, weight was a concern. This multifunction table served as a pad, that keeps track of diaper changes, and a scale. My favorite features: it is heated and easy to clean. Blowouts? Simple wipe down.

7. Burp Cloths (More Than Just Fabric)

Comfy Cubs Burp Cloth Set: These are basically your new best friend. Buy extras. Buy SO many extras. But then in every drawer and corner of your house. You'll be doing laundry anyway.

8. Laundry Pods (Throw and Go)

9. Onesies in Multiple Sizes (Because Babies Grow Overnight)

Gerber Organic Onesies: You need newborn, 0-3 months, and 3-6 months. Tight onesies=blowouts. My favorite style- kimono. I have yet to meet a baby who likes things out over their heads. Simply lay your baby on the onesie, and button away.

10. A Baby Monitor (Your New Parenting Superpower)

MomCozy:This closed circuit baby monitor made me feel protected from predators speaking to my babe. Also, it's easy to link up multiple cameras if you need them in multiple rooms.

The Essentials Philosophy

The key to a good baby registry is this: Don't buy what you think you need—buy what you'll actually use. Every baby is different, but these 10 items will make the first few months significantly less chaotic.

And remember: babies don't care about fancy stuff. They care about clean diapers, food, and cuddles. Everything else is just making your life easier. Which is what we're going for, right?
