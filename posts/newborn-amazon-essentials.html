The Essentials Philosophy

The key to a good baby registry is this: Don't buy what you think you need—buy what you'll actually use. Every baby is different, but these 10 items will make the first few months significantly less chaotic.

And remember: babies don't care about fancy stuff. They care about clean diapers, food, and cuddles. Everything else is just making your life easier. Which is what we're going for, right?
