What Is an Affiliate Link?

An affiliate link is a special URL that tracks when you click through to a product on Amazon. If you make a purchase after clicking an affiliate link, we earn a small commission at no additional cost to you.

Amazon Associates Participation

Mom Life Picks is a participant in the Amazon Associates Program, an affiliate advertising program designed to provide a means for sites to earn advertising fees by advertising and linking to amazon.com.

Disclosure Statement: As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases.

All product links on Mom Life Picks that direct to Amazon are affiliate links. When you click these links and make a qualifying purchase on Amazon, Mom Life Picks earns a commission.

How Affiliate Links Work

  1. You click on a product link on Mom Life Picks
  2. You're redirected to Amazon.com
  3. If you make a purchase within the Amazon session, we earn a commission
  4. You pay the same price—there are no added fees or markups
  5. Your purchase helps support Mom Life Picks

Identifying Affiliate Links

Most product links on Mom Life Picks are affiliate links. If you're unsure whether a link is an affiliate link, you can:
